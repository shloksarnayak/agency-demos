The call that goes unanswered mid-shift, texted back before the caller hangs up

Your BBB profile shows a couple of customer inquiries that never got a reply, not because anyone dropped the ball on purpose, but because calls and messages land somewhere nobody's watching while the crew is mid-pour or mid-drive. This is a working example of a missed-call text-back and intake system: the phone rings, nobody picks up, the caller gets an instant text instead of a dial tone, and the job details are waiting in a queue by the time someone's back in the truck.

What I'd flag before this touches a real phone number

Nothing here actually sends a text. A real version connects to a texting line (the same number on your trucks and site signs works, or a forwarding number) so the auto-reply and job details move over SMS the moment a call is missed, not through a demo page.
This isn't a replacement for the crew calling back. It's the same as picking up the phone, just automatic and instant: the caller gets acknowledged and the job details get written down, so nothing sits unanswered until someone's back at a desk.
Service list and area are copied from usapave.com today. Sealcoating, crack sealing, patching, resurfacing, and full-depth paving across Chardon, Chagrin Falls, Burton, Middlefield, and the surrounding area — if that list changes, this needs a one-line update to match.
The lead inbox here is a static illustration. A real version would live wherever the crew already looks: a shared inbox, a group text, or a simple dashboard, not a separate tool to remember to check.
