Working example, built for The Tank Man

Reschedule a visit without calling the same line a new lead calls

The Tank Man runs recurring Weekly, Bi-Weekly, and Monthly aquarium maintenance routes, but today every one of those regular clients has to book, confirm, or reschedule a visit through the same inbox or phone number a first-time caller would use. This is a prototype of a client-facing alternative: an existing route client sees their next visit and reschedules it in a couple of clicks, gets an automated route-day reminder with a one-tap reschedule link, and a first-time visitor asking about service lands in a separate, clearly labeled inquiry form instead of the same queue.

Everything on this page is a fictional sample account and fictional visit data. No real client names, addresses, prices, or schedule data from The Tank Man are used here. A public website or Google Business listing for this business could not be located during research, so the plan structure (Weekly / Bi-Weekly / Monthly) and the single-inbox booking gap shown below are taken directly from the description in the outreach email sent to thetankman05@yahoo.com, not from a public listing. This prototype is not connected to that inbox, phone number, or any live system.
