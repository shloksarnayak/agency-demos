A generator install isn't done until the monitoring account is proven to work.

Wired Electrical & Generators (also doing business as Generators By Wired, Tomball TX) is a certified Generac dealer with an A+ BBB rating and over a thousand positive reviews, this is a well-regarded shop, not a business in distress. But its own BBB complaint file shows a specific, recurring pattern: a customer charged for monitoring that was never properly registered, and a separate case describing a delayed response after a post-hurricane generator failure. Both trace to the same two moments: whether the Generac Mobile Link enrollment actually transferred to the customer's own account before the crew left, and whether a customer waiting during a storm-driven surge in calls gets any update at all. This example shows both pieces working end to end.

Why this, specifically

Targets a documented gap, not a guess. Two independently-reviewed BBB complaints describe a monitoring enrollment charged without proper registration and a delayed post-hurricane response, this tool addresses exactly those two moments.
Does not overstate the problem. Wired Electrical & Generators has an A+ BBB rating and predominantly positive reviews. This is a narrow process add-on to a well-run shop, not a rescue plan.
Frames the fix as a verification step. Generac's own Mobile Link pairing is independently documented as a common industry-wide setup snag, so the checklist is about proving the handoff happened, not implying the hardware is at fault.
