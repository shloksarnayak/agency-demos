What I'd flag before this goes anywhere near a real job

The 14-day no-visit stall threshold and 14-day credit/refund overdue threshold are placeholders. A real build uses whatever response-time commitment your Warranty Specialist and Director of Operations actually want to hold the office to.
This is an alert layer, not a new system of record. It sits alongside however warranty tickets, site visits, and invoices already get tracked across the Sarasota HQ and the Waterside Sales Center, it doesn't replace scheduling, permitting, or your existing Finance Operations workflow.
The charge sign-off gate only catches one mistake. Sending an invoice or re-charge tied to a company-caused defect, a failed inspection, a mis-sited excavation, before someone internally reviews it. It doesn't touch estimating, contracts, or payment processing.
The underlying construction-quality issues aren't something this tool fixes. A misaligned paver, a siting error, or a failed inspection is a workmanship or process problem. This tool only closes the gap where the follow-up, or the charge tied to the mistake, goes unnoticed by the office until a customer escalates.
