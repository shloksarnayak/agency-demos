What I'd flag before this goes anywhere near a real job

This checklist is a starting point, not your final one. It's built from the defect pattern in your public reviews and BBB record, a real rollout should be checked against what your own crew leads and project managers already know goes wrong.
It doesn't replace your estimate, scheduling, or invoicing process. This sits as a gate in front of "mark complete," it doesn't touch how a job gets quoted, scheduled across your four offices, or billed.
"Overdue" here is a placeholder threshold (3 days). A real build uses whatever response-time commitment you want to hold yourselves to, and can route by office instead of pooling all four into one list.
Nothing here emails or calls a customer. It's an internal tracking tool for your team; it doesn't change what the customer sees or send anything on your behalf.
