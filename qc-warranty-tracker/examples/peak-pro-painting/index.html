What I'd flag before this goes anywhere near a real job

This checklist is a starting point, not your final one. It's built from the coverage/streaking pattern in your public reviews and BBB record, a real rollout should be checked against what your own crew leads and the Office Administrator already know goes wrong on site.
It doesn't replace your estimate, scheduling, or invoicing process. This sits as a gate in front of "mark complete," it doesn't touch how a job gets quoted, scheduled across Denver, Austin, and Dallas, or billed.
"Overdue" here is a placeholder threshold (5 business days). A real build uses whatever response-time commitment you want to hold yourselves to for the 2-year warranty, and can route by market instead of pooling all three into one list.
Nothing here emails or calls a customer. It's an internal tracking tool for your team; it doesn't change what the customer sees or send anything on your behalf.
