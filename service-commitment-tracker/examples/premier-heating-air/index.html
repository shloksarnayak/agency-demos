Premier Heating and Air: a queue that catches a missed maintenance visit before it becomes a fourth cancelled call

Premier's own site (checked directly) already runs a working "Schedule Now" form and an "Instant Quote" tool, with no ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Jobber, FieldEdge, or Calendly signature detected anywhere in the page code, and no customer-facing account or visit-history view on any fetched page. What's missing sits above that initial booking: nothing tracks whether a promised visit, an install follow-up or one of a paid maintenance plan's contracted annual visits, actually happens once it is on the calendar. Premier's own BBB complaint and review file documents what that gap looks like in practice: paid installation and maintenance-plan visits repeatedly rescheduled or missed by the company, with no proactive tracking of what is still owed, before it turns into a formal complaint. This example shows a small internal queue built for Premier's CSR and dispatch staff: every promised visit gets a due date and a cancellation count, and a visit that is cancelled twice or sits overdue escalates itself into a manager queue automatically.

How this gets built for real

The core deliverable is the office and manager queues, not a customer portal. The researched offer here is an internal follow-through tool for CSR/dispatch staff and management. The customer lookup tab above is a small optional extra, not a replacement for Premier's existing "Schedule Now" form.
This does not touch quoting or sales conduct. Premier's own complaint file also shows a separate pattern about high-dollar replacement recommendations; that is a trust and sales-practice question, not a scheduling gap, and this tool deliberately does not claim to fix it.
The reference code is whatever the office already uses. A real build reads from an existing work-order or maintenance-plan account number instead of asking a customer to remember a new code.
The customer view shows only customer-safe wording. Internal notes and manager escalation reasons typed in the office and manager views never appear on the lookup page.
