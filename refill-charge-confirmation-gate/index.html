Confirm the price before the vial fills, not after the card is charged

Any pharmacy or recurring-dispensing business that keeps a card on file for automatic refills eventually gets the same complaint: a shipment arrives, a charge posts, and the patient says they never saw the amount coming, sometimes because the day supply changed, sometimes because it was their first fill on that medication. Once a prescription has left the pharmacy it legally cannot be returned to stock, so the dispute can only be prevented before the fill, not resolved after it. This page shows one small, working tool that does that: every refill whose amount or day supply differs from the patient's last charge, or has no prior cycle to compare against, is held out of the charge queue until the patient actively confirms, cancels, or skips it, and a cycle that matches what was charged last time clears automatically. Every action is timestamped and logged, instead of depending on someone reading a support inbox.

Everything below is fictional. When this gets built for a real business, it reads the fill queue and the last-charged amount from whatever dispensing and payment system is already in place, for example a pharmacy order queue and a card-on-file payment gateway, and adds the hold-and-confirm step and the audit log on top of that, without replacing the dispensing system itself.

Refill pre-charge queue

Every upcoming refill, with what was charged last cycle next to what this cycle would charge. A cycle that differs, or has no prior cycle to compare against, is held and flagged instead of moving to the charge queue on its own.

Pre-charge gate check

Today's batch of charges about to reach the payment gateway, checked against what each patient actually confirmed. Nothing posts on its own say-so.

Audit log

Every notice, confirmation, cancellation, and skip, timestamped as it happens.

Confirm, cancel, or skip a refill

Try RX-1003, RX-1004 or RX-1005 from the pharmacy view's sample queue. RX-1001 and RX-1002 have not had a notice sent yet, so they will not be found here until the pharmacy view sends one.

How this gets built for real

The notice is sent from the pharmacy view here. A real build sends a text or email automatically the moment the fill queue produces a cycle that differs from the patient's last charge, or has no prior cycle to compare against.
The pre-charge gate check compares two things the business already has. What the patient actually confirmed, and what the fill queue is about to charge. It sits between the dispensing queue and the payment gateway, it does not replace either.
Confirming, canceling, or skipping is logged the moment it happens. A real build keeps this in the same audit trail a signed intake form would produce, this demo shows the workflow, not a specific messaging or e-signature vendor.
