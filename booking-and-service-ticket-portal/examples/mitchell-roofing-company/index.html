Mitchell Roofing: a real slot to book, and the scope, materials and price confirmed in writing before work starts

Mitchell Roofing Company, LLC is a 12-year, family-owned, licensed Florida contractor (CCC1330496) serving six counties around Saint Petersburg, GAF and Owens Corning certified, A- BBB accredited, and rated 4.8 out of 5 across 706 Google reviews, a genuinely strong overall reputation. Its own site (homepage, contact page, about page and privacy policy all checked directly) routes every inspection request through a phone number and one generic contact form, no online scheduling anywhere. Its public BBB complaint record shows a recurring pattern underneath that strong overall rating: several disputes trace back to something agreed verbally that was never put in writing, not workmanship in isolation. This example shows a real inspection/estimate booking calendar, plus a job ticket that cannot move forward until the scope, materials and price are confirmed in writing and visible to the customer, and a status trail so a customer mid-project is not reduced to unanswered calls.

1. Self-service inspection & estimate booking calendar

Matches what PITCH Roofing and Roofing.Co, two named local competitors, already offer: a real date-and-time-slot picker for a free inspection or storm-damage assessment, instead of Mitchell Roofing's current phone-or-contact-form-only intake.

Hours and days shown are illustrative for this example. A real build uses Mitchell Roofing's own published hours, which were not found written out on their current site.

2. Job ticket, scope & price confirmation, and status tracker

Directly targets the BBB pattern: every job becomes a visible ticket with an assigned crew lead and ETA, moves through a real status trail instead of going silent, and cannot be marked scheduled until the materials, color and price have all been confirmed in writing here, the exact step that was missing on the vent-color dispute and the undisclosed coastal warranty limitation.

How this gets built for real

The calendar is a standalone log here. A real build reads and writes whatever schedule Mitchell Roofing's own crews already use, sitting alongside the existing WordPress site (its Roof Triage Tool and Savings Estimator stay exactly as they are), so staff see one schedule, not a second one to keep in sync.
Booking confirmation is on-screen and in local storage only. A real build sends an actual confirmation email or text and can be embedded directly into the existing WordPress site.
The scope-and-price gate is a field, not a payment or e-signature system. It only needs to record one thing per job: what materials, color and price were the customer told in writing, so that record is checkable if a dispute like the vent-color one ever comes up again. A real deployment would add an actual typed-name/checkbox acknowledgment step on top of this.
