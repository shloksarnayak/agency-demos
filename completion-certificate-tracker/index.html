Every job leaves a paper trail, automatically

Many service businesses promise documentation, a certificate, a receipt, a confirmed count, but deliver it manually, per job, if at all. When it slips, customers are left unable to confirm the work was done, disputes over quantity go unresolved, and a promised callback quietly never happens. This page uses a fictional business to show a job-completion flow that generates the certificate itself, catches count mismatches before they become billing disputes, and keeps flagged follow-ups visible until someone closes them.

Everything below is fictional. When this gets built for a real business, it plugs into however jobs are actually logged today, a driver's phone, a dispatch sheet, a simple form, and adds the certificate, the count check, and the follow-up flag on top, without replacing how the job itself gets done.

How this gets built for real

Reads from wherever jobs are already tracked. This demo's form is a stand-in. A real build pulls from the driver's existing intake step, a phone form, a dispatch sheet, a scheduling tool, rather than adding a second place to log the same job.
The certificate is the deliverable, not a generic PDF. It's generated per job the moment it's marked complete, not assembled by hand later, closing the gap between what a business promises on its own site and what a customer actually receives.
