A course isn't done until the certification is confirmed submitted

Two BBB complaints from 2022 describe divers who paid $1,069.90, passed every requirement, and still never received their PADI certification paperwork, both still marked unanswered. With scheduling, gear, and travel bookings largely handled by one person, it's an easy step to lose once a course wraps up. This working example shows the fix: every completed course stays visibly open, flagged for follow-up, until someone confirms the PADI submission went through and logs the confirmation number, so a paid certification never quietly falls through the cracks again.

BBB complaint audit

A quick check of ATA's own BBB profile, run as part of this build:

Two complaints (2022): paid students, requirements passed, PADI paperwork never receivedBoth marked unanswered, no tracker to confirm submission ever happened

Both complaints describe the same underlying gap: nothing in the shop's process forces a completed course to stay visible until the PADI submission is confirmed. That is exactly the kind of promise this tracker is built for: logged the moment a course wraps up, and visible until it's submitted and confirmed, instead of relying on a paying customer to notice their certification never arrived.

How this gets built for real

Plugs into the existing sign-off process. Instructors already confirm when a student finishes their final dive or exam, this adds the PADI submission step to that same moment, it doesn't replace how courses are taught or scheduled today.
Submission becomes a tracked step, not a memory. Nothing closes as "done" until someone marks it submitted to PADI with a confirmation number, closing the exact gap behind both BBB complaints.
