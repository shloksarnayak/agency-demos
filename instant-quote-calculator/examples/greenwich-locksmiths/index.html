What I'd flag before this goes live

The dollar figures are placeholders. Greenwich Locksmiths' own site advertises "affordable rates" and "free estimates" but doesn't publish a rate table, so every price here is marked "Illustrative" and needs your real numbers by job type before this replaces a phone quote.
This doesn't decide what a job actually needs. A lockout can turn into a lock change once someone's on-site, or a rekey can turn out simpler than expected. The point isn't to lock in a final price sight unseen, it's to give a starting number and a range so nobody's first time hearing a figure is after the work's already started.
The arrival-window text is a scripted simulation, not a working phone number. Standing this up for real means a provider like Twilio wired to whatever dispatch tool your technicians already use, so the window updates automatically instead of needing someone to send it by hand.
This calculates and demonstrates, it doesn't book anything. No calendar, no payment, no confirmation email. The honest next step after "here's your estimate" is still your existing quote and dispatch process, this just puts a number and a window in front of the customer earlier.
