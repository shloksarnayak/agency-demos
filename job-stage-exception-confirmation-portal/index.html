FFixpoint Studio Templates / Job-stage & exception confirmation portal
Template, not built for a specific business

No measurement, fee, or schedule change reaches the customer after the fact

Businesses that fabricate or install a custom, high-ticket product, stone, cabinetry, custom furniture, signage, share a common failure mode: the job moves through measurement, production and install with no customer-facing status, and any deviation from the original quote, an added or oversized-item fee, a schedule slip, a mid-job scope change, surfaces only on the final invoice or in a phone dispute instead of before it happens. This page shows one small, working tool that closes both gaps: a stage timeline the customer can check anytime, and an exception gate that blocks the job from moving to its next stage until the customer explicitly reviews and confirms the change with a typed name and timestamp.

Everything below is fictional. When this gets built for a real business, it reads from whatever already tracks a signed quote or work order (a CRM, a spreadsheet, an intake form) and connects the notification pings to the business's existing email or text system.

How this gets built for real

The reference number is whatever the business already gives a customer. A real build reads from the signed quote or work-order number already in use, it doesn't invent a new one to remember.
The notification pings here are log entries, not real sends. A real build wires the "stage updated" and "exception needs your confirmation" pings to the business's existing email or SMS system so they go out the moment staff log the event.
"Confirmed" here is a typed name and a timestamp. A real build can add an actual e-signature provider for a stronger legal record on a fee or scope change; this demo shows the workflow, not a specific vendor.
The gate blocks the next stage, not just a charge. A real build can hold a card charge, a crew dispatch, or both, on top of blocking the stage advance, depending on what the business needs held.
