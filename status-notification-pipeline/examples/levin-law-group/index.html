What I'd flag before you roll this out anywhere else

Nothing here sends a real email or text. This demo only shows what a client and any co-counsel, lender, or agent on the file would receive. A real version needs an actual email/SMS service (for example SendGrid or Twilio) wired to whatever field your paralegals already update to mark a file's stage.
This sits beside your existing client-file portal, it doesn't replace it. Clients who prefer to log in and pull up documents still can. This only adds the automatic push half your reviews say is missing, so a client stops finding out about a status change only by calling, emailing, or thinking to check the portal.
The milestone stages shown for a purchase/sale closing and for an estate matter are typical for NY residential real estate and probate work, not copied from your firm's internal case-management process. A real build should confirm the exact stages your team already tracks and the field they update to move a file between them.
Worth a specific conversation before this goes live: what a status update can safely say. Since this touches active legal matters, a real build should keep the message content to a status label and a plain next step (as shown below), not case substance, so nothing privileged or confidential goes out over an unencrypted text.
The BBB and Birdeye reviews above are real and quoted directly, this is a reasonable response to the documented pattern, not a guarantee nothing is ever delayed. A title search can still take longer than expected. What changes is whether the client, and whoever else is on the file, hears about it automatically instead of finding out by calling repeatedly or being told only after the fact.
