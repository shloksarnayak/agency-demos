Working example, built for SM Lawson Solicitors

A matter update that goes out on its own, and a concern that can't quietly disappear

SM Lawson Solicitors' own Trustpilot page carries six reviews, all one star, five of them naming slow or absent communication directly: a time-sensitive conveyancing matter where important emails went unanswered and a subsequent complaint was dismissed rather than properly investigated, a client told someone would call back who never did, no follow-through on timelines given, and weeks of silence over a disputed bill. This example shows what a client sees instead: a self-serve status check by matter reference, across all three of the firm's offices, and a concern that is logged, dated, and stays visibly open until a fee earner formally closes it.

What's real: SM Lawson Solicitors is a recognised sole practice (SRA no. 618617) since 01/11/2015, authorised for all legal services, with three SRA-regulated solicitors (Julian Peter William Fallows, Kathryn Ann Price, and Principal Shazu Miah) across three offices: Kidderminster (head office), Tipton, and Worcester. A direct fetch of the firm's own Trustpilot page found a 2.3 out of 5 TrustScore from six reviews, all one star and organic (the page itself notes the firm has not invited customer reviews). Five of the six name poor or slow communication specifically: Masood Saeed (10 Dec 2025) describes a time-sensitive conveyancing matter where important emails went unanswered, and says a subsequent complaint was dismissed rather than properly investigated; Andrew Downes (14 Feb 2024) describes zero communication and being told someone would call back, which never happened; R M (11 Oct 2023) describes no follow-through on the timelines given; James (5 Oct 2021) describes weeks of no progress and a disputed bill; Philip Robinson (30 Jul 2025) gives no further detail. A second, independent panel on solicitors.com shows 17 reviews averaging 2.8 out of 5, consistent with the Trustpilot pattern, though that page doesn't break out individual review text. A third platform, ReviewSolicitors, was reported via search at 2.0 out of 5 from four reviews, directionally consistent but not independently re-fetched this session, so it's flagged here as the weaker, secondary source. A direct fetch of smlawson.co.uk found no client portal, case tracker, or matter-status lookup of any kind, only phone, email, a general contact form, and live chat. No SRA or Solicitors Disciplinary Tribunal record exists for the firm or for Shazu Miah. UK legal-tech products such as Clio (from roughly £49-59/user/month) and The Access Group's Legal client portal already market exactly this kind of case-tracking and client-portal capability to firms of this size.

What I'd flag before you roll this out anywhere else

Nothing here sends a real email, text, or letter. This demo only shows what a client would receive. A real version needs an actual email/SMS service, for example SendGrid or Twilio, wired to whatever field your fee earners already update across the three offices to mark a matter's stage.
The concern channel directly answers the Trustpilot review describing a complaint that was "dismissed rather than properly investigated." When a client raises a concern from the lookup screen, it is timestamped and logged immediately as a visible item in the fee earner view, and it stays open until a fee earner takes the explicit "log response sent" action below. It cannot quietly disappear or close itself. I deliberately did not attach a specific promised response time to this: no SM Lawson-specific complaints-handling target turned up in research, so a real build should use your actual written complaints procedure and real target dates, not a guessed one.
Matters are tagged by office, Kidderminster, Tipton, or Worcester, so a client or the firm can see at a glance which of the three sites is handling a file. Nothing on smlawson.co.uk today, phone, email, the contact form, or live chat, surfaces that.
The stages shown for conveyancing and for wills and probate are typical for that kind of work, they are not copied from SM Lawson's actual case-management process. A real build should confirm the exact stages used across all six of the firm's practice areas (conveyancing, wills and probate, family law, child proceedings, personal injury, litigation, and immigration) directly with Shazu Miah.
The 2.3 out of 5 Trustpilot score (six reviews) and the solicitors.com figure (2.8 out of 5, 17 reviews) are both real and convergent, and the firm has not solicited reviews on either platform, but both are small samples, and the firm carries no SRA or Solicitors Disciplinary Tribunal record. This reads as a real but fixable communication and complaints-handling gap on a decade-old, actively regulated small practice, not a sign of fraud or total failure. The ReviewSolicitors figure (2.0 out of 5, four reviews) is reported, not independently re-verified this session, and is the weakest of the three sources above.
This introduces visibility your site doesn't have today, it doesn't replace or sit beside an existing tool. A direct review of smlawson.co.uk found no client portal, case-status login, or tracking feature of any kind, only phone, email, a contact form, and live chat, plus a free 30-minute initial consultation offer.
