The visit update a family shouldn't have to call and ask for

What I'd flag before you roll this out anywhere else

Nothing here sends a real text or email. This demo shows exactly what a family member would receive at each step. A real version needs an actual SMS/email service (for example Twilio or SendGrid) connected to whatever your Care Coordinator already uses to confirm caregiver shifts.
The four stages shown (visit scheduled, caregiver confirmed, visit in progress, visit complete) are a typical home-care visit flow, not copied from your internal process. Worth a short conversation with your Care Coordinator before this gets built for real, your team may track shift confirmation differently.
The "only a phone call away" language and coordinator role structure are quoted and described directly from your live site, the gap is a reasonable read of what's publicly visible, not a claim about what happens on calls we haven't heard. Families may already get informal updates by phone that don't show up on your website.
The family request form only captures a schedule question or update request, it doesn't book a specific visit. Keeping it to "what's needed and how to reach you" is deliberate, matching your current phone/contact-form intake rather than promising a live scheduling calendar this demo doesn't have.
Visit notes shown below (e.g. "assisted with morning routine, blood pressure checked") are fictional placeholders. A real build only surfaces whatever level of detail your caregivers and RN are comfortable sharing with family, and stays within any HIPAA-related handling your agency already follows.
