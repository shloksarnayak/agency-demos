What I'd flag before this gets built for real

Nothing here sends a real text or email, and no money actually moves. This demo shows exactly what a customer would receive at each production milestone, and exactly what happens the moment a refund is approved. A real version needs an actual SMS/email service connected to your production tracking, and a real integration with whatever processor issued the original charge (Stripe, Square, PayPal, or your InkSoft-linked processor).
The five stages shown (order received, artwork approved, on press, QC & packing, shipped) are a typical screen-printing production flow, not copied from your internal process. Worth a short conversation before this gets built for real — your team may track a job differently across five presses running that many hours a day.
The BBB reviews are real and quoted directly with names and dates as published; the fix is a reasonable response to that documented pattern, not a guarantee every order ships on time. A part of your backlog will still run behind sometimes. What this changes is whether a customer hears about it automatically, and whether an approved refund gets stuck behind a manual step in a shared sales inbox.
The intake form only captures a request, it doesn't verify order details against a real database. Keeping it to "what's wrong and how to reach you" is deliberate, this demo isn't connected to any real order records.
