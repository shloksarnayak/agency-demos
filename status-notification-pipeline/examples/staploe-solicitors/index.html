The progress update that doesn't wait for a client to call

Staploe Solicitors LLP's own Trustpilot page shows a "Poor" TrustScore (2.6 out of 5 across 28 reviews); six independent reviewers, spread across thirteen months and as recent as six weeks ago, describe the same thing: emails and phone calls going unanswered, promised callbacks that never arrive, and having to repeatedly chase for any progress update, including one reviewer who had to chase separately just to get documents released after the matter had already completed. This example shows what that looks like fixed: every matter carries a visible "last updated" marker, a matter that goes quiet past a set number of days is automatically flagged for the fee earner, and a client can check a matter's status, or request their post-completion documents once a matter is concluded, without needing anyone to pick up the phone.

What I'd flag before you roll this out anywhere else

Nothing here sends a real email or text. This demo only shows what a client would receive. A real version needs an actual email/SMS service, for example SendGrid or Twilio, wired to whatever field your fee earners already update to mark a matter's stage.
The feature this firm specifically needs is the quiet-matter flag, not just a stage-change notice. Six of your own Trustpilot reviews describe unanswered emails and calls and broken callback promises, not a matter that simply moved slowly. Below, every matter shows when it was last updated, and once a matter passes the no-update threshold without any contact, an "overdue for an update" flag appears automatically in the fee earner view, before a client has to call and ask. A fee earner can clear it by advancing the matter or by sending a plain check-in, even when nothing has actually changed yet, which a basic stage-advance notice alone cannot do.
The post-completion document request closes a specific, separate gap one of your reviewers described. Lotusdreamer's review describes having to chase again, after the matter had already completed, just to get documents released. Below, once a matter reaches "Concluded," the client view shows a "Request my post-completion documents" button; using it logs an immediate acknowledgment with a committed turnaround and surfaces a dedicated "Issue post-completion documents" action in the fee earner view, so that request can't quietly sit unanswered the way the review describes.
This introduces visibility your site doesn't have today, it doesn't sit beside an existing tool. A direct fetch of staploesolicitors.com found no client portal, case tracker, or matter-status lookup anywhere on the site; fee queries and case questions are handled entirely by phone, email, or a contact form. This closes the exact gap the reviews describe rather than adding a second way to do the same thing.
The matter types shown span your actual mixed practice, not just conveyancing. The sample below includes a wills and probate matter, a residential property purchase, a business debt recovery matter, and an employment matter, reflecting the practice areas listed on your own site, rather than assuming this is a single-practice firm the way a pure conveyancing tracker would.
Worth a specific conversation before this goes live: what a status update or check-in can safely say. A real build should keep message content to a status label and a plain next step, as shown below, never matter substance, so nothing privileged goes out over an unencrypted text or email.
The Trustpilot score above is real and cited directly, and it is one unclaimed, self-selecting sample, not Staploe's full client base. A separate review platform, ReviewSolicitors, shows a more favourable 4.2 out of 5 across roughly 60 reviews for this same firm, a genuine, disclosed mixed signal rather than a uniformly poor record. What a quiet-matter flag and a self-serve document request change is whether a gap in communication gets caught and fixed automatically, not whether every file is currently being mishandled.
