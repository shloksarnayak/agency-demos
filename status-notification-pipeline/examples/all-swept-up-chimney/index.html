The cancellation alert that would have stopped a customer waiting on a no-show technician

An Angi reviewer wrote: "I only found out about the cancellation when I called because no one showed up. Had I not called they would not have come, even though there was a scheduled appointment." This page shows the specific piece that closes that gap: the moment a booking is cancelled or its schedule changes, the customer gets a text automatically, without having to call and find out the hard way.

What I'd flag before this goes further

Nothing here sends a real text or email. This demo shows exactly what a customer would receive at each step. A real version needs an actual SMS/email service (for example Twilio or SendGrid) connected to the Jobber account they already have, either through Jobber's API or a webhook triggered when a job's status changes.
The four stages shown (booked, confirmed, technician en route, complete) are a typical sweep/inspection flow, not copied from All Swept Up's internal Jobber setup. A gas log service call, a new installation, or an animal removal job may move through this differently, worth a short conversation before this gets built for real.
Cancellation notice is a separate, dedicated action from rescheduling, on purpose. The Angi complaint was specifically about a cancellation nobody communicated, not a reschedule. This demo keeps the two distinct: a reschedule tells the customer a new window, a cancellation tells them the visit isn't happening and gives a reason, so nobody is left waiting on a technician who was never coming.
The service-request form only captures a request, it doesn't book a specific time slot. Keeping it to "what you need and how to reach you" mirrors what the existing contact form already collects, just routed somewhere that triggers a confirmation instead of sitting in an inbox.
The BBB rating, Jobber site, and both reviews quoted are pulled from public listings today; they can shift. Worth confirming against Jobber and the live site before anything here gets built for real.
