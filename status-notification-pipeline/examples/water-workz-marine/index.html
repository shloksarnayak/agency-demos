What I'd flag before you roll this out anywhere else

Nothing here sends a real text or email. This demo shows exactly what a customer would receive at each step. A real version needs an actual SMS/email service (for example Twilio or SendGrid) connected to whichever system your service desk already uses to mark a ticket as diagnosing, waiting on parts, or ready for pickup.
The six-stage flow shown (dropped off, diagnosing, waiting on parts, in progress, ready for pickup, picked up) is a typical marine-service flow, not copied from your internal process. Worth a short conversation before this gets built for real, your service team may track things differently or add a step like a signed estimate approval.
The queue position shown at drop-off is a count, not a promised date. It answers "how many boats are ahead of mine" without promising a specific day, since that's the actual information a customer is given verbally today, just automatically and the moment it changes instead of only when they call.
The "report issue persists" action models the Birdeye review directly, it is not a guarantee every recheck gets fixed. What it changes is that a reopened issue becomes a tracked, front-of-queue ticket with its own notification, rather than a phone call that may or may not get written down anywhere.
The BBB and Birdeye reviews are real and quoted directly; the detailed Birdeye account is from several years ago and your BBB complaint count is low relative to your review volume. This is a reasonable response to a documented, real pattern, not a claim that the problem is currently severe or widespread, your dealer rating overall is strong.
