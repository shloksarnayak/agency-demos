The update that goes out before the customer has to call back

A Trustpilot reviewer, Javier Martin, wrote that staff were "extremely unprofessional...they never cared" and stopped "answering phone or replying messages." Another reviewer, Catherine, wrote that when she tried to place a follow-up order, "no one had bothered to respond" across multiple calls and emails. A third, Bianca M, described a squeaky-staircase snag after installation going unanswered across multiple contact attempts. All three describe the same gap: once a staircase order is placed and paid for, nothing tells the customer what's happening or confirms their message was even received, unless they chase it themselves.

What's real: Stair and Banister Ltd (Companies House #12297705, active, incorporated 4 November 2019, sole director Jake Millar) describes its own process on stairandbanister.co.uk as on-site measurement, CAD drawing, in-house manufacture, delivery, and one-day installation. Trustpilot shows 29 reviews, 4.2/5 overall, 86% five-star, with the three reviews quoted above (Javier Martin, 7 Oct 2022; Catherine, 5 Sep 2022; Bianca M, 4 May 2025) describing the same stop-responding-after-the-sale pattern. A direct fetch of the live homepage found no portal, client login, order-status tracker, or dashboard of any kind, just phone numbers and the info@stairandbanister.co.uk inbox. Products that solve exactly this kind of job-visibility gap for joiners and trade businesses, Trade2Base among them, are already sold and marketed today.

What I'd flag before you roll this out anywhere else

Nothing here sends a real email or text. This demo only shows what a customer would receive. A real version needs an actual email/SMS service (for example SendGrid or Twilio) wired to the info@stairandbanister.co.uk inbox and to whatever your team already uses to mark an order's stage.
The five stages shown (measurement booked, CAD design sent for approval, in manufacture, installation scheduled, installed) match the process described on your own site, they are not copied from your internal job tracking. Worth a short conversation before this gets built for real, to confirm the exact point your team marks a job as moved.
The "Flag a snag" button is built directly from the Bianca M review. Try it on the installed sample order below: instead of a customer having to call and call again about a squeaky step, they get an automatic acknowledgment the moment the office logs it, and a visible status once it's booked in and resolved.
No portal, login, or order-status tool exists on stairandbanister.co.uk today, confirmed by a direct fetch of the live homepage. This is a new capability, not a second system competing with something that already works.
The reviews above are real and quoted directly, this is a reasonable response to the documented pattern, not a guarantee every job goes perfectly or every snag is fixed instantly. A squeaky step or a delayed manufacture slot can still happen. What this changes is whether the customer hears something automatically, the moment they make contact and at each real step after, instead of calling repeatedly and getting nothing back.
