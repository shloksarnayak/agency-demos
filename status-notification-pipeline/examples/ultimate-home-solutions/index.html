The acknowledgment that goes out every time, even on the third recall

A Trustpilot reviewer, Saffron Smedley, wrote that a shower leaked six months after installation and "they can't even call me back." Four separately dated reviewers, the same shape every time: a job gets done, a snag gets reported, and the trail goes cold, often by the second or third contact.

What's real: Ultimate Home Solutions Limited (Companies House #SC442380, active, sole director Colin Fraser Pass, trading from a showroom at Dobbies Garden Centre Braehead, Renfrew) installs fitted bathrooms, kitchens, windows, doors and roofing across Central Scotland. Trustpilot shows 299 reviews, a 4.3 TrustScore, 85% five-star. Among the visible one-star reviews, five independently named and dated reviewers across eighteen months (Saffron Smedley, Ellen, Alice, Letitia Campbell, Zuzka M) describe the same pattern quoted above: the job is substantially complete, a snag or follow-up is reported, and calls and messages then go unanswered, in some cases across multiple recall attempts. Several real UK job-management products (Tradify, Commusoft, Powered Now) already sell customer-facing job-status and communication features that answer exactly this kind of gap.

What I'd flag before you roll this out anywhere else

Nothing here sends a real email or text. This demo only shows what a customer would receive. A real version needs an actual email/SMS service (for example SendGrid or Twilio) wired to the info@ultimategroup.co.uk inbox and to whatever your team already uses to mark a job's stage.
The five stages shown (survey and design confirmed, materials ordered, installation scheduled, installed, snag/callback window open) are a reasonable generic fit for a design-supply-install business, not copied from your internal job tracking. The company's own site could not be directly fetched for this demo (its SiteGround hosting blocks automated fetches), so this needs a short conversation to confirm the exact point your team marks a job as moved.
The "Flag a snag" button is built directly from the Saffron Smedley, Alice and Zuzka M reviews, and it can be used more than once per job. Try it on the sample job already in the snag/callback window below: log a snag, then log a second one on the same job. Each one gets its own automatic acknowledgment with a reply-by date, which is exactly the point where the real reviews describe the company going quiet.
No client portal, login or job-status tool was found for Ultimate Home Solutions on the independent sources checked (press coverage, trade directories, a trade-profile listing), only a phone number and showroom visits. This is weaker evidence than a direct fetch of the live homepage, which the site's own bot protection blocked, disclosed here rather than overstated. This would be a new capability either way, not a second system competing with something that already works.
The reviews above are real and quoted directly, this is a reasonable response to the documented pattern, not a guarantee every job goes perfectly or every snag is fixed instantly. A leak or a delayed recall can still happen. What this changes is whether the customer hears something automatically, on the first contact and every one after it, instead of calling and emailing repeatedly and getting nothing back.
