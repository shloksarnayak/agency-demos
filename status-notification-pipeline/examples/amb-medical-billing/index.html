The claim update a patient shouldn't have to call and ask for

AMB runs billing for family practices, surgeons, physical therapists, chiropractors, massage therapists, and other providers on a contingency basis, "we don't get paid unless you do." Two BBB complaints filed about two weeks apart earlier this year describe the same pattern: calls and emails going unanswered while a patient tried to get a receipt or a billing update. AMB operates on phone, fax, and email, with no online way for a patient to check a claim's status or pull their own receipt without calling in.

Two BBB complaints filed roughly two weeks apart earlier this year both describe calls and emails going unanswered while a patient tried to get a receipt or billing update, and no self-serve claim/receipt status option appears anywhere online for AMB.

What I'd flag before this gets built for real

Nothing here sends a real text or email. This demo shows exactly what a patient would receive at each step. A real version needs an actual SMS/email service (for example Twilio or SendGrid) connected to whatever system AMB's billing staff already use to track a claim.
The four stages shown (claim submitted, insurance review, payment posted, receipt ready) are a typical medical billing claim flow, not copied from AMB's internal process. Worth a short conversation with AMB's billing staff before this gets built for real, the actual claim lifecycle may have more steps (denials, appeals, secondary insurance) than this demo shows.
The contingency model, 30-plus-year history, and provider types served are quoted and described directly from amb5280.com, and the BBB complaint pattern is from two filed complaints. The gap described (no self-serve status option) is a reasonable read of what's publicly visible, not a claim about what happens on calls we haven't heard.
The patient request form only captures a billing question and how to reach the patient back, it doesn't process a payment or dispute a charge. Keeping it to "what's needed and how to reach you" is deliberate, matching AMB's current phone/email intake rather than promising a live payment portal this demo doesn't have.
Claim numbers, patient names, practice names, and every dollar figure below are fictional placeholders, not real patient or billing data. A real build only surfaces whatever a patient is already entitled to see about their own account, and stays within HIPAA-related handling AMB already follows.
