The progress update, and the revised quote, that don't wait for a client to call

Sydney Mitchell LLP's own Trustpilot page shows a "Poor" TrustScore (2.0 out of 5 across 17 reviews, 41 percent one star). Reviewers across nearly six years, four of them in the last seven months, describe the same thing: calls and emails that go unanswered and no proactive update on a live matter, including one reviewer left waiting six weeks for a revised cost estimate he had specifically asked for. This example shows what that looks like fixed: every matter carries a visible "last updated" marker, a matter that goes quiet past a set number of days is automatically flagged for the fee earner, and a client can check a matter's status, or request a revised cost estimate, without needing anyone to pick up the phone.

What I'd flag before you roll this out anywhere else

Nothing here sends a real email or text. This demo only shows what a client would receive. A real version needs an actual email/SMS service, for example SendGrid or Twilio, wired to whatever field your fee earners already update to mark a matter's stage.
The feature this firm specifically needs is the quiet-matter flag, not just a stage-change notice. Four of your own Trustpilot reviews from the last seven months describe calls and emails going unanswered and no proactive update, not a matter that simply moved slowly. Below, every matter shows when it was last updated, and once a matter passes the no-update threshold without any contact, an "overdue for an update" flag appears automatically in the fee earner view, before a client has to call and ask. A fee earner can clear it by advancing the matter or by sending a plain check-in, even when nothing has actually changed yet, which a basic stage-advance notice alone cannot do.
The revised cost estimate request closes a specific, separate gap one of your reviewers described. Richard Leek's review describes unclear billing and a six week wait for a revised quote he had specifically asked for, and the Legal Ombudsman's own decision against this firm (D012390) separately found excessive cost. Below, a client can use a "Request a revised cost estimate" action on any open matter; using it logs an immediate acknowledgment with a committed turnaround and surfaces a dedicated "Issue revised cost estimate" action in the fee earner view, so that request can't quietly sit unanswered for six weeks the way the review describes.
This introduces visibility your site doesn't have today, it doesn't sit beside an existing tool. A direct fetch of sydneymitchell.co.uk found no client portal, case tracker, or matter-status lookup anywhere on the site; fee queries and case questions are handled entirely by phone, email, or a contact form. This closes the exact gap the reviews describe rather than adding a second way to do the same thing.
