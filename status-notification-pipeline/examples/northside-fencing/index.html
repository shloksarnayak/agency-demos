The warranty claim that gets an answer, not a second one gone quiet

A ProductReview.com.au reviewer, Olga, wrote that her fence was built below the agreed height with shorter gates than ordered, and "he stated he will not fix anything." Another, Kaz, wrote that a warranty claim on a fence over $20,000 went unanswered and that she was told she was banned from requesting future work after complaining, so she filed a complaint with Queensland Fair Trading. A third, harry h., wrote that a mains electrical line drilled during installation failed nine months later and "they don't care." Independently dated reviewers describe the same shape: the fence goes up, something is wrong or fails later, and the business stops answering, sometimes refusing the work outright.

What's real: Northside Fencing (QLD) Pty. Ltd. (ABN 45 060 593 259 / ACN 060 593 259, active on the Australian Business Register since 24 March 2000) manufactures and powder-coats pool, garden, timber, Colorbond, weldmesh and glass fencing from a single Burpengary, Queensland premises housing its office, showroom, workshop, powder-coating factory and timber yard. What I'd flag before you roll this out anywhere else

Nothing here sends a real email or text. This demo only shows what a customer would receive. A real version needs an actual email/SMS service (for example SendGrid or Twilio) wired to the sales@northsidefencing.com.au inbox and to whatever your team already uses to mark a job's stage.
The five stages shown (survey booked, in manufacture/powder-coating, installation scheduled, installed, warranty/callback window open) are a reasonable fit for a fencing manufacturer that builds and powder-coats its own product, not copied from your internal job tracking. A direct fetch of northsidefencing.com.au today found no mention of a customer login, portal, or job-status page of any kind, so this is a new capability, but worth a short conversation to confirm the exact point your team marks a job as moved.
The "File a warranty/callback claim" button is built directly from the Olga, Kaz, Karen and harry h. reviews, and it can be used more than once per job. Try it on the sample job already in the warranty/callback window below: log a claim, then log a second one on the same job. Each one gets its own automatic acknowledgment with a reply-by date, tracked separately until it is marked resolved, which is exactly the point where the real reviews describe the business going quiet or refusing to continue.
No portal, login, or warranty-tracking tool exists on northsidefencing.com.au today, confirmed by a direct fetch of the live homepage. Products already sold to other fencing and trades businesses (Nexus Service Manager, WorkGuru, Jobber, ClickHome) show this kind of tracking is an established, sellable pattern, not a speculative feature.
The reviews above are real and quoted directly, this is a reasonable response to the documented pattern, not a guarantee every job goes perfectly or every claim is resolved instantly. A defect or a delayed manufacture slot can still happen. What this changes is whether the customer hears something automatically, on the first claim and every one after it, instead of calling and emailing repeatedly and getting nothing back, or being told not to come back.
