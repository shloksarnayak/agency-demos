The update that goes out the moment something changes

Many service businesses only tell a customer where their job stands when the customer calls to ask. This page uses two fictional jobs to show the opposite: the team marks a job's status, and the customer sees exactly what they'd have received automatically, no call needed.

Everything below is fictional. When this gets built for a real business, it connects to whatever system their team already uses to mark a job's status, and sends through a real SMS/email service, the way the example below shows.
