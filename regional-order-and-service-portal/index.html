Template, not built for a specific business

One portal, every hub, three kinds of request

Multi-location delivery and service businesses often run every kind of customer request through the same single "request a quote" callback form, whether it's a standing weekly order, a same-day urgent request, or a piece of equipment that needs a repair. This page shows a self-serve alternative: a customer picks the location that serves them, then submits the right kind of request through its own short form, and it lands automatically in that location's queue.

Everything below is fictional. "Example Regional Supply Co." is not a real business. When this gets built for a real one, the hub list, product/service catalog, and hours are drawn from what that business actually publishes, the way the Tennessee Valley Ice example below does.

How this gets built for real

The hub list and catalog are the real work. The generic locations and products here are placeholders; a real build starts from that business's actual locations, products or service types, and hours.
Intake and routing, not full dispatch or billing. This template deliberately stays narrow: request intake, hub routing, and a shared queue view. Route optimization, invoicing and payment are separate scope.
