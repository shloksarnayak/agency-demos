Hot Tubs, Inc.: a place for every promised warranty visit, callback, or paperwork step to live until it's kept

Hot Tubs, Inc.'s own BBB complaint file and its BBB customer reviews describe the same repeated shape: a warranty visit, a callback, a parts delivery, or a finance paperwork filing is promised for a specific time, and it then goes untracked long enough that the customer has to chase the company from zero, sometimes for months. The business has no dedicated service or customer-portal page on its own site today. This example shows a small tool that gives every open promise a due time, an automatic overdue flag sorted worst-first for the office, and a reference-code lookup so a customer can check status without starting the call over.

How this gets built for real

This replaces nothing, it fills a real gap. The company's own site has no service, portal, or order-tracking page today, so a real build gives the office its first shared view of open promises instead of competing with an existing system.
The reference code is whatever the office already writes down. A real build reads from an existing work order, service ticket, or invoice number instead of inventing a new one.
The status lookup shows only customer-safe wording. Internal notes typed in the office view stay in the office view, so staff can look up a call without exposing internal handling notes.
