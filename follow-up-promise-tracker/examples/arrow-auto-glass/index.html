Arrow Auto Glass: a visible queue for every callback, warranty claim, and reschedule the office owes a customer

Arrow's own site (a WordPress build, checked directly) runs entirely on phone-based scheduling, including a confirmation call the morning of service, with no online booking, no customer portal, and no way for staff to see at a glance which promised callbacks or warranty decisions are still outstanding. The company's public BBB complaint file shows what that gap can look like in practice: a complaint from May 2024, after an April 2024 windshield replacement, cites reported window-tint damage where the customer says "manager never returned call," and a separate complaint from July 2024 describes a warranty that was initially honored, then retracted, before a partial reimbursement was worked out. This example shows a small internal tool built for office staff: a queue of every open commitment (a callback owed, a warranty claim pending, a reschedule needed), sorted by how overdue it is, so nothing depends on one person remembering.

How this gets built for real

The core deliverable is the office queue, not a customer portal. The researched offer here is an internal commitment tracker for staff. The customer lookup tab above is a small optional extra, not a replacement for Arrow's existing phone-based scheduling.
The reference code can be whatever the office already writes down. A real build reads from an existing work-order or claim reference instead of asking a customer to remember a new code.
This does not replace scheduling. It sits alongside Arrow's existing phone-based booking and only tracks the specific commitment: a callback, a warranty claim, or a reschedule.
The customer view shows only customer-safe wording. Internal notes typed in the office view never appear on the lookup page.
