Dallas Automatic Gate: a place for every promised estimate or scheduled visit to live until it's kept

Two different review platforms, months apart, describe the same shape: an estimate or a scheduled repair visit gets promised, and then nothing happens until the customer has to chase it themselves. A GuildQuality review from October 2025 describes an estimate promised within three days that never arrived, with the gate still non-functional. Separately, Angi reviews describe a customer needing three service visits over one week without resolution, citing a "lack of communication regarding the date of work." This example shows a small tool that gives every open estimate or scheduled visit a due time, an automatic overdue flag, and a place a rep can check without starting the whole conversation over.

How this gets built for real

This sits beside the existing contact form, not instead of it. A real build tracks what happens after a request comes in, an estimate promised or a visit scheduled, it does not replace how new requests get submitted.
The reference code is whatever the office already writes down. A real build reads from an existing job or estimate number instead of inventing a new one, so nothing has to be re-keyed.
The status lookup shows only customer-safe wording. Internal notes typed in the office view stay in the office view, so a rep can look up a job without exposing internal handling notes.
