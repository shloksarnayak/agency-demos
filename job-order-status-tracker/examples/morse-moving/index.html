A booked move shouldn't go quiet while a customer waits to hear where it stands

A dated, quoted BBB review (Romulus listing, December 2023) describes asking Morse Moving & Storage to fix a problem and getting no reply at all: "I was met with silence." Separately, Great Guys Moving's own aggregated scoring of 684 reviews for this company breaks out Conflict Handling at 2.02/10 (90.3% of coded reviews negative) and Communication at 5.21/10 (56.7% negative). The company's own website today lists phone numbers, branded office locations and a "Get a Free Quote" form, and nothing else: no client login, shipment-tracking page or move-status portal anywhere on the site, the same gap Atlas Van Lines closes for its own customers with its "Atlas Portal" order-status tool. Below is a working tool sized to close that specific gap: every booked move gets a reference that moves through Quote confirmed, Crew / truck assigned, Loading day, In transit and Delivery scheduled through to Delivered, shows plainly what it is currently waiting on and the next expected milestone date, and flags automatically any move that has gone quiet too long, instead of only surfacing once a customer calls or writes in to chase.

Everything below is fictional. Customer names, move details and dates on this page are sample data, illustrative only, not Morse Moving & Storage's real customer or job files. One sample move below is deliberately shaped to mirror the "went silent after being asked to fix a problem" pattern described in the BBB review quoted above, to make the "needs attention" flag concrete, not because it reflects a real Morse Moving & Storage customer or job. This tool is scoped to move-status visibility only; it does not address pricing, quoting or billing, a separate, disclosed issue this page makes no claim about. Morse Moving & Storage also carries many positive reviews praising punctuality and experienced drivers; this page targets only the narrower status-visibility gap evidenced above, not a verdict on the company's overall service.

How this gets built for real

The move reference is whatever Morse already uses on file. A real build reads from the company's existing job/order number across every branded location instead of inventing a new one for the customer to remember.
The status update here is a log entry, not a real send. A real build wires this to the company's existing email or text system so the update actually reaches the customer the moment a stage changes or a milestone date is set, instead of the customer having to call or write in to find out.
This does not replace dispatch's own judgment on a move. It only makes a move that has gone quiet, waiting on a crew assignment, a loading confirmation or a delivery window, visible to the whole team automatically, instead of surfacing only when a customer calls to chase or complains.
Scoped narrowly on purpose. This tool covers move-status visibility only, across local, long-distance and commercial moves alike. It does not touch pricing, quoting or billing; that is a separate, disclosed issue and out of scope here.
