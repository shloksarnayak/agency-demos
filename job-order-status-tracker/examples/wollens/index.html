A matter shouldn't go quiet while a client waits to hear where it stands

The UK Legal Ombudsman's own public decision-data register lists Wollen Michelmore LLP (trading as Wollens) with two final decisions on record, one of which required a remedy for the client. Separately, on the firm's own Trustpilot profile, several dated one-star reviews across 2025 and 2026 describe the same theme in their own words: "do nothing but delay, delay and cause issues and obstructions at every stage" (Steve, 22 Oct 2025); "Work and replies at a snails pace if reply at all" (Mr Hart, 14 Oct 2025); "Error after error and delay after delay" (Mark Laurenti, 11 Jul 2025, firm refunded fees after acknowledging poor service). The firm's own website today lists phone numbers, office addresses for its three Devon offices (Torquay, Exeter and Barnstaple) and a contact form, and nothing else: no client login, case-tracking portal or matter-status page anywhere on the site. Below is a working tool sized to close that specific gap: every open matter, across conveyancing, family, private client/probate and commercial work alike, gets a reference that moves through Instructed, Searches / documents pending, Awaiting third party and Next milestone scheduled through to Resolved, shows plainly what it is currently waiting on and the next expected milestone date, and flags automatically any matter that has gone quiet too long, instead of only surfacing once a client calls or writes in to chase.

Everything below is fictional. Client names, matter details and dates on this page are sample data, illustrative only, not Wollens's real client or case files. One sample matter below is deliberately shaped to mirror the delay pattern described in the public Trustpilot reviews quoted above, to make the "needs attention" flag concrete, not because it reflects a real Wollens client or file. This tool is scoped to matter-status visibility only; it does not address complaints handling itself or give legal advice, and makes no claim about the outcome of any individual matter. Wollens also carries many 4-5 star reviews praising fast, clear communication; this page targets only the narrower status-visibility gap evidenced above, not a verdict on the firm's overall service or legal competence.

How this gets built for real

The matter reference is whatever Wollens already uses on file. A real build reads from the firm's existing case/file reference across all three offices instead of inventing a new one for the client to remember.
The status update here is a log entry, not a real send. A real build wires this to the firm's existing email system so the update actually reaches the client the moment a stage changes or a milestone date is set, instead of the client having to call or write in to find out.
This does not replace a fee earner's own professional judgment on a file. It only makes a matter that has gone quiet, waiting on a search, a third party or the other side, visible to the whole team automatically, instead of surfacing only when a client calls to chase or complains.
Scoped narrowly on purpose. This tool covers matter-status visibility only, across any practice area (conveyancing, family, private client/probate, commercial property). It does not touch complaints handling, legal advice, or billing; those need a different fix and are out of scope here.
