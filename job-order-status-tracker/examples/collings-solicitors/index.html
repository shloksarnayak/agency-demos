A conveyancing matter shouldn't go quiet while a client waits to hear where it stands

The UK Legal Ombudsman's own public decision-data register records a finding against Collings Law Limited (decision D012310, dated 09/03/2026, residential conveyancing): Delay, Failure to respond, Failure to keep informed, Failure to progress, and poor complaints handling, with compensation awarded for distress and disruption. The firm's own website today offers phone, fax, email and a contact form, and nothing else: no client login, case portal or status page of any kind. Below is a working tool sized to close that specific gap: every open matter gets a ticket that moves through Instructed, ID & documents received, Searches ordered, Searches / mortgage offer received, Contract agreed and Exchange of contracts, through to Completion, a simulated status update fires on each stage change, what the matter is currently waiting on is shown plainly rather than buried in a file note, and any matter stalled too long at a stage is flagged automatically instead of only surfacing once a client calls to chase.

Everything below is fictional. Client names, matter details and dates on this page are sample data, illustrative only, not Collings Law's real client or case files. One sample matter below is deliberately shaped to mirror the delay/failure-to-progress pattern recorded in the public Ombudsman decision, to make the "needs attention" flag concrete, not because it reflects a real Collings Law client or file. This tool is scoped to case-status visibility only; it does not address complaints-handling process itself or give legal advice, and makes no claim about the outcome of any individual matter.

How this gets built for real

The matter reference is whatever Collings Law already uses on file. A real build reads from the firm's existing case/file reference instead of inventing a new one for the client to remember.
The status update here is a log entry, not a real send. A real build wires this to the firm's existing Microsoft 365 / Outlook email so the update actually reaches the client the moment a stage changes, instead of the client having to call to find out.
This does not replace a fee earner's own professional judgment on a file. It only makes a matter that has gone quiet, waiting on a search, a mortgage offer or the other side, visible to the whole team automatically, instead of surfacing only when a client calls to chase or complains.
Scoped narrowly on purpose. This tool covers case-status visibility only. It does not touch complaints handling, legal advice, or billing, those need a different fix and are out of scope here.
