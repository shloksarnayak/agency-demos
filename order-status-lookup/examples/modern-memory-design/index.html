What I'd flag before you roll this out anywhere else

Only the two sample order references above return anything. This isn't connected to your real order system, so it can't look up an actual customer's frame. A real version would need a link to however your shop currently tracks a job, even a whiteboard or a shared spreadsheet.
The four stages shown (order received, in production, quality check, ready) are a typical custom-framing flow, not copied from your internal process. Worth a short conversation about how your shop actually phases a job before this gets built for real.
This is a status check, not a claims or refund system. The damaged-goods and refund complaint needs a person to handle either way, this only covers the routine "where's my frame" question, so a delayed or damaged order doesn't sit silent for weeks by default.
This only shows status. It doesn't change your turnaround time, it just means a customer waiting past your published 4-6 days gets an automatic update instead of no answer to their calls.
