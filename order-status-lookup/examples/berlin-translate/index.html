A page where a translation order can be checked, instead of chased

Public reviews for Berlin Translate describe the same problem repeatedly: a customer left unsure whether their document had shipped, especially for certified and notarized translations where proof of mailing genuinely matters. This is what a self-serve order status page plus automatic stage emails would look like, sitting on top of the order reference you'd already give each customer at intake.

What I'd flag before you roll this out anywhere else

Only the three sample references above return anything. This demo isn't connected to your real order records, it can't look up an actual customer's translation. A real version would need a link to wherever your team currently logs orders (illustrative stage names below, not copied from your internal process).
The reviews are real, the fix is a reasonable response, not a guarantee. Your public reviews show a repeating pattern of customers unable to get a status update without chasing, particularly around certified and notarized copies sent by post. A self-service lookup plus automatic stage emails addresses that specific pattern. It doesn't speed up translation or notarization itself, it means a customer isn't left wondering whether their document was ever mailed.
The five stages shown (order received, translation in progress, certified/notarized, printed & signed, dispatched) are a typical certified-translation order flow, not your internal process. Worth a short conversation before this gets built for real, especially since document type (certified translation vs. sworn/notarized copy vs. proofreading) changes the steps.
"Check status" is the only action here. It doesn't let a customer amend an order, upload a document, or message your team. Keeping the first version narrow is deliberate, those can be added once the lookup itself is proven useful.
