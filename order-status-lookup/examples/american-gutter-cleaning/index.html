Proof a cleaning happened, before the invoice does

Your site has an online estimate form and a "Pay Online" link, but nothing that confirms a scheduled cleaning actually happened. Two BBB complaints filed within two weeks of each other in December 2023 point at the same underlying gap: no record either side could point to when a billing dispute came up. Here's what an automatic photo-and-timestamp confirmation, tied to the invoice, could look like.

What's real: your live site has no service-confirmation system, customer portal, or completion record anywhere, checked directly today. BBB shows two complaints filed within two weeks of each other (Dec 17 and Dec 31, 2023). The Dec 31 complaint describes a customer who "discovered a full year of accumulated debris despite being billed," leading to roughly $3,000 in water-damage repairs, where the company's response disputed the timeline but had no service record to settle it either way. The Dec 17 complaint is a different situation, a customer billed after what the company itself called a miscommunication over whether a visit had been authorized at all.

What I'd be straight with you about: these are two different disputes, not one repeating pattern, and I'm not claiming most jobs go undocumented. What they share is that in both cases, once a customer questioned a bill, there was nothing recorded to check the story against. A completion confirmation directly addresses the Dec 31 case (was the visit actually done), and gives your team a record to point to generally, though it wouldn't by itself have resolved a dispute about whether a visit was authorized in the first place. It doesn't take a side, it just means a dispute like this has something to check against.
