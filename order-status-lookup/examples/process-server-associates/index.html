Closing the gap after the first attempt email

Your site already emails a client when a job is attempted or when service is complete, that part isn't missing. What Yelp reviews describe is what happens in between: a case that needs a second or third attempt, or runs into trouble, goes quiet after that first email until the client calls in. Here's what an update at each additional attempt could look like, on top of what you've already built.

What's real: your site's own copy says clients "get email notification when your job has been attempted or when service is complete," and you already log GPS coordinates, timestamps, and snapshots for every job in a client portal, both checked directly today. Three separate Yelp reviewers, different years, describe the same follow-on pattern: after that first "attempted" email, nothing further goes out automatically, so when a case needs more than one attempt, the client has to call or email repeatedly to find out what's happening.

What I'd be straight with you about: three Yelp reviews aren't proof this happens on most jobs, plenty presumably complete on the first attempt with no gap to notice. What they point at is specific: the notification you already send covers the two milestones at the ends of a case, attempted and complete, but a case that runs longer than that has no automatic update in between. A competitor, Proof, markets "live updates as attempts occur" as a selling point, which is exactly that middle stretch.

What's illustrative: the two sample case references below aren't real cases. They show what typing a reference into a working version of this page would return, using the data your own portal copy says you already log per job. A real build would connect to your existing case system rather than duplicate it.
