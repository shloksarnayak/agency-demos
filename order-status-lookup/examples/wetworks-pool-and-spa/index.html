What I'd flag before you roll this out anywhere else

Only the two sample project references above return anything. This isn't connected to your real jobs, so it can't look up an actual customer's build. A real version would need a link to however your team currently tracks project stages, even if that's just a shared spreadsheet.
The stages shown (design/permitting, materials ordered, construction, final walkthrough) are a typical pool-build flow, not copied from your internal process. An 18-month water-feature project likely has more stages than a standard pool remodel. Worth a short conversation about how your team actually phases a job before this gets built for real.
This is meant for active, multi-week projects, not quick service calls. A same-day repair doesn't need a status page, this is specifically for the kind of long build where "where are we" is a real, recurring question.
This only shows status. It doesn't replace a phone call when something's actually gone wrong, it just means a routine check-in doesn't have to become one.
