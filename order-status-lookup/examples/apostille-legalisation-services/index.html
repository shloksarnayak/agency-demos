What I'd flag before you roll this out anywhere else

Only the three sample references above return anything. This demo isn't connected to your real order records, it can't look up an actual customer's document. A real version would need a link to wherever your team currently logs orders (Illustrative stage names below, not copied from your internal process).
The reviews are real, the fix is a reasonable response, not a guarantee. Your public reviews show a repeating pattern of customers unable to get a status update without chasing. A self-service lookup plus automatic stage emails addresses that specific pattern. It doesn't speed up the FCO or an embassy, it means a customer isn't left wondering whether anyone is working on their document.
The five stages shown (received, ID/documents checked, sent for legalisation, apostilled/stamped, dispatched) are a typical apostille order flow, not your internal process. Worth a short conversation before this gets built for real, especially since document type (apostille vs. embassy legalisation vs. translation) changes the steps.
"Check status" is the only action here. It doesn't let a customer amend an order, upload a document, or message your team. Keeping the first version narrow is deliberate, those can be added once the lookup itself is proven useful.
