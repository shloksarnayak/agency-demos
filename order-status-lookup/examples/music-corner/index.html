A hire status that doesn't depend on someone remembering to reply

Your hire process runs on GoCardless for payments and DPD for delivery and returns, but nothing tells a customer where their hire actually stands in between. Trustpilot reviews, at different points in the hire lifecycle, dispatch, refund, and general contact, describe the same underlying gap: no automatic update, only silence until someone calls in. Here's what an automatic hire-status update could look like.

What's real: your site runs hire payments through GoCardless and delivery/returns through DPD, checked directly today, with no automated status tool connecting the two. Trustpilot reviews describe a dispatch that took three weeks with no update except when the customer called in, a deposit refund chased from January to May 2024 with no response, and a more recent example of a saxophone deposit refund promised within two weeks that then took three unanswered follow-ups to actually resolve.

What I'd be straight with you about: this is a handful of dated reviews, not a claim that most hires go wrong, and I'd frame this as a question worth asking rather than something I'm certain is an active problem. What's notable is that the same gap, no automatic update, shows up at different points in the hire lifecycle rather than in one bad month, which suggests it's structural rather than a one-off. An automatic status update doesn't speed up a refund, it just means the customer isn't left wondering whether anyone's looking at it.

What's illustrative: the two sample hire references below aren't real hires. They show what typing a reference into a working version of this page would return, using the stages your own process already describes, confirmed, dispatched, returned, deposit handled. A real build would connect to your existing GoCardless and DPD accounts.

What I'd flag before you roll this out anywhere else

Only the two sample hire references above return anything. This isn't connected to your real GoCardless or DPD accounts, so it can't look up an actual customer's hire. A real version would need read access to both.
The four stages shown (hire confirmed, dispatched, return received, deposit processed) follow your own published process, not an invented one. Still worth a short conversation to confirm this matches how your team actually tracks a hire day to day.
This is a status check, not a refund or dispatch system. Deciding a refund still needs a person, this only means the customer gets an automatic update instead of silence while that decision is made.
This only shows status. It doesn't change how quickly a refund gets processed, it just means the customer isn't the one who has to chase for an update.
