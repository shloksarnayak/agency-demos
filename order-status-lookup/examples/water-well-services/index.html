What I'd flag before you roll this out anywhere else

Only the two sample job references above return anything. This isn't connected to your real Jobber account, so it can't look up an actual customer's job. A real version would use Jobber's own data, since you already have it.
The four stages shown (dispatch confirmed, technician en route with an arrival window, on site, complete) are a typical well-service flow, not copied from your internal process. Worth a short conversation about how your dispatchers actually phase a call before this gets built for real.
