What I'd flag before this goes anywhere near a real season

Status and renewal visibility only, not scheduling or dispatch. This doesn't reassign crews or send anything, it gives your office one place to check instead of a phone call or a spreadsheet.
Real stages, matching how your team actually tracks a job. The four-stage flow here is a placeholder, a real build asks how repairs and takedowns are actually logged today.
Seasonal timing. This is worth building ahead of the Oct-Jan season, not during it, so it's ready when the repair calls start.
