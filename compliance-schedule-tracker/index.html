Track who's due, before the client has to ask

Many code-mandated recurring services (fire-code cleanings, safety inspections, equipment servicing) are still run off a phone and a paper calendar, with nothing that flags a client's next due date before it's missed. This page uses fictional accounts to show what a due-date board and a structured intake request look like.

Everything below is fictional. When this gets built for a real business, "send reminder" would connect to however the business actually contacts clients (email, text). Nothing on this page sends a real message to anyone, it only logs a simulated timestamp in your browser.
