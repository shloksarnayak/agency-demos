Why this, specifically, for DRS Hoods

Targets a documented failure mode. A dated customer account (found on DRS Hoods' own legacy-name reviews page) describes a missed appointment, an unresolved follow-up, and no proactive rescheduling, exactly what a due-date board with reminders is built to catch before it happens.
Multi-location accounts stay grouped. One reviewer independently confirmed a relationship spanning all of a client's locations. The "Sample Multi-Unit Restaurant Group" row below shows that pattern, one account, one row, tracked as a whole rather than as separate untracked jobs.
A real, nearby competitor already does this. HOODZ of Orlando, a franchise operating in DRS Hoods' own listed Orlando service area, advertises "state-of-the-art scheduling software" as a differentiator. DRS Hoods' own live site has no scheduling or CRM software at all.
Back to the generic pattern: recurring service compliance scheduler template.
