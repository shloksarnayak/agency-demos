Two properties on one account, two due dates, tracked separately

Accurate Backflow Testing & Plumbing already has online booking and payment for a single visit, that part works and isn't being reinvented here. What a single-job booking widget doesn't do is track a multi-property account, where each address has its own county-mandated annual retest date. This page shows what a per-property compliance calendar looks like, using fictional sample accounts.

Per-property due-date board (fictional sample accounts)

Each account can hold more than one property. Every property keeps its own last-test date and its own due date, scheduling one doesn't touch the other.

New property / test record

Fills the role of the phone call or online booking Accurate Backflow gets today. Type an existing account name to add a second property to that same account, or a new name to start one. The next required test date is computed automatically from the last test date, not typed in by hand.

Why this, specifically, for Accurate Backflow

Targets a documented failure mode. A BBB complaint (8/20/2024) describes a customer with two properties on the same account. Scheduling and testing the first property did not carry over to the second, its test lapsed, and the county issued late fees. Accurate Backflow's own reply to the complaint confirms the facts, it disputes only who should have called. The board above models exactly that shape of account, one name, two properties, two independent due dates.
Doesn't duplicate what already works. Accurate Backflow's live site already has single-job online booking and payment, real customer reviews describe it as easy. This demo doesn't re-pitch that, it fills the specific gap a single-job booking flow leaves open: nothing that watches a multi-property account as a whole.
Comparable competitors already offer this. Lee Backflow (Lee County, FL) markets support for "HOAs, multi-unit properties, commercial sites, and facilities with multiple backflow assemblies" with annual reminders. A Plus Backflow Test advertises bulk scheduling and annual reminders for HOA/property managers. Accurate Backflow's own site shows no equivalent multi-property tracking today.
Back to the generic pattern: recurring service compliance scheduler template.
