Pull up the record before you charge the fee again

A direct fetch of Best Septic Tank Pumping's own site (homepage, services, about-us, contact-us) confirms no CRM, online booking, or customer portal of any kind, only a contact form and phone number. Two BBB complaints on file point at the same underlying gap: a customer was charged a $450 locate fee for a tank the company had already located twice before (2011 and 2018), and a separate complaint over an unexpected $750 line-clearing charge sat with no recorded business response, the specific pattern BBB cites for the account's current "C" rating. This working example shows the fix: a property lookup that surfaces prior locate and pump-out dates before a new charge goes out, an automatic pump-out reminder built from that same history, and a complaint log where a filed issue stays visibly open, with a growing day count, until a response is logged against it.
