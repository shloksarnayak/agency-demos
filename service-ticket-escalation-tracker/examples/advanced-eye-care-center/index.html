A billing dispute should not go quiet for six months

Advanced Eye Care Center's own BBB file (Denton, TX) includes a complaint filed October 23, 2024: a patient called before a March 5, 2024 appointment to confirm the office accepted their new insurance, was billed $127.52 as out-of-network anyway, then emailed the office twice (6/26/24 and 7/10/24, the second bounced back as if the address was blocked), and mailed a paper letter, all with no response, until the account was sent to collections. The BBB record shows no business response was ever filed. This example adds a stage-tracked billing dispute queue behind the office's existing phone and email intake, so a dispute that has gone unanswered for a set number of days surfaces automatically instead of relying on someone remembering to check back.

How this maps to Advanced Eye Care Center specifically

Stages mirror what actually happened in the BBB complaint. Logged, first response sent, second follow-up (patient escalates), escalated internally, resolved. The AEC-1001 sample dispute below models the real timeline: initial call before the appointment, a charge the patient disputed, two follow-up emails (the second effectively undeliverable), and no internal escalation before it aged into collections.
The overdue window shown here (14 days sitting in one stage) is illustrative. The real complaint describes gaps of weeks between contact attempts and a total unanswered period of several months; the real cutoff would be set with Advanced Eye Care's billing team, not guessed.
Notifications here are a log entry only, nothing is actually sent. A real build would confirm receipt and each status change to the patient automatically, through the office's existing phone/email intake, so a second or third follow-up from the patient's side is never the only way to get a response.
No stakeholder name is used because none was publicly confirmed. Advanced Eye Care Center's own site lists four treating clinicians (Lawrence A. Deliberately narrow in scope. This tool tracks and escalates the status of a billing dispute once it's logged; it does not adjudicate insurance network status or determine who owes what.
