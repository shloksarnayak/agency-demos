What I'd flag before this goes anywhere near a real order

This never places or finalizes an order. Every line below either gets an explicit yes from a person, or gets kicked to "needs a look." Nothing here writes to your order system, touches real stock, or emails anyone.
It only knows this 41-item slice. Anything from the rest of your catalog comes back as "not found" here, not because you don't carry it, just because this demo wasn't given the rest of it. A real build reads whatever catalog export or data feed you already keep current, so it knows everything you actually stock.
Where your team actually keys orders in is the open question. Whether that's an ERP, a phone order pad, EDI, or something else changes how this plugs in. Worth a short conversation before anything gets built for real, this is a demo of the matching step, not a finished integration.
Pricing shown is what your site displayed when we checked, not a live quote. A few items on your own site don't show a price without adding to cart, that shows up below as "matched, call to confirm price," never a guessed number.
