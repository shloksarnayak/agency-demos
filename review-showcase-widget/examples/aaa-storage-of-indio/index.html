What I'd flag before this goes further

The review text and tenant list here are labeled samples. I don't have access to your Google Business, Yelp, or Cubby accounts, so nothing below is a real review or a real tenant. A real build pulls actual reviews through Google's and Yelp's own APIs, and actual tenant/lease data through Cubby's export or API, not a scrape or a guess.
Posting the response is still your call, always. This drafts a reply and stages a text, it never auto-posts to Google or auto-sends an SMS on its own. A real build can flag anything unusual (a 1–2 star review, a tenant who mentions moving out) for you to review before it goes out.
This doesn't touch your Cubby account itself. It sits on top of it: Cubby stays the system of record for rentals and billing, this layer is specifically the outward-facing follow-up — responding publicly and reaching tenants directly — that nothing in Cubby automates today.
