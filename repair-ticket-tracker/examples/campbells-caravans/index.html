What I'd flag before this goes further

Only the four sample reference codes above return anything. This isn't connected to a real workshop job or a real warranty case. A real build would read from whatever Campbells already uses to track a booked-in job or a warranty query, likely a workshop diary or DMS job-card system across both sites, rather than a new system for staff to keep updated separately.
Automatic SMS/email pings are simulated as an internal log entry here, not actually sent. A real build wires each stage change to an SMS or email the moment staff mark it, which is exactly the gap the reviews describe: being told someone would call back and then not hearing anything for weeks or months.
Each ticket here carries a staff-set target completion date, and the overdue flag is a straight comparison against today's date. A real build would let Campbells set that date from whatever it already promises a customer at drop-off or first contact, rather than this placeholder being typed in free-form.
Two departments are scoped here on purpose: a workshop job (booking in through collection) and an aftersales/warranty issue (query through resolution), because those are the two distinct complaint patterns found in the reviews, not an assumption about how the business is actually organized internally. Both Lostock Hall/Preston and Blackburn are represented in the sample tickets' own descriptions, a real build would likely add a site filter alongside the department one.
