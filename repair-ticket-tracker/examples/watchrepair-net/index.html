What I'd flag before this goes further

Only the four sample reference codes above return anything. This isn't connected to your real bench log, so it can't look up an actual customer's watch. A real version would need to read from whatever your team already uses to note a watch's stage.
The BBB complaint and review pattern are real and multi-source, the fix is a reasonable response, not a guarantee. A self-service lookup addresses the specific "nobody told me" pattern. It does not make a genuinely delayed repair faster, it means the customer and the office both see it before it becomes a six-week silence.
The five stages shown (received, diagnosed, in repair, quality check, shipped) are a typical mail-in watch repair flow, not copied from your internal process. Your real bench stages might be named differently, worth a short conversation before this gets built for real.
No plain-text email could be verified on your site, every address is Cloudflare-obfuscated, so a real build's automatic customer pings would need to go by SMS or through whatever intake channel you confirm works.
