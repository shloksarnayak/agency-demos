Right now, checking on a mailed-in watch means calling or emailing and waiting

Your own site offers a free assessment, a free insured postage label, a quote before work starts, and insured return shipping, a real mail-order repair model, not a walk-in counter. But once a watch arrives, there is no self-service way for a customer to see where it stands. The only tracking mentioned on your site is the carrier's postage tracking, which shows where the parcel travelled, not what stage the repair itself is at. That gap matches a specific pattern across several independently-written Trustpilot reviews: watches held for weeks to months with calls unanswered and emails that go unreplied, in some cases on genuinely valuable pieces.

What's real: I checked your site directly, there is no account login, customer portal, or repair-status lookup anywhere on it, only "tracked postage labels" for the outbound/return parcel. Five separately-written 1-2 star reviews describe the same shape: a watch held for weeks to months, with calls that go unanswered and emails that get no reply. I could only corroborate this on one review platform (flagged honestly, not a two-platform pattern), but five independent reviewers describing the identical symptom is a real signal, not a single complaint.
