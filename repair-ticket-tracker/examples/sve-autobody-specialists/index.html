What I'd flag before this goes further

Only the four sample claims above return anything. This is not connected to your real intake, it cannot look up an actual customer's vehicle. A real version would read from the same free-estimate form you already run, so a claim record exists the moment a car is checked in.
The four to five call pattern is real but thin, one documented review out of hundreds of positive ones, and it was read through a third party mirror since Yelp blocks direct fetching of its own reviews. Worth confirming directly on Yelp before it is referenced anywhere client facing. The fix on offer here is meant to sit alongside the personal updates Tom, Lena and Cailee already give, not replace them with something automated and impersonal.
The seven stages shown are a typical collision repair flow, not your literal internal process. Your real stage names and order may differ, worth a short conversation before this gets built for real.
Phone plus last name is the customer's main path in, claim number is the fallback. A real build would also need a rule for what happens with two customers sharing a last name and phone on file, a small detail worth deciding up front rather than after launch.
